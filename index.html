Method. Endpoint list from fal's model index (categories text-to-video + image-to-video, all pages). Duration, resolution, aspect-ratio, frame-count, audio and start/end-frame fields read from each endpoint's published openapi.json. Pricing from fal's own per-model pricing copy, normalised to a 60-second 720p render.

Read $/min as a rate, not a shopping cart. Most of these models cap out at 5–15 seconds, so a full minute is not a single generation — the column exists to make wildly different billing units (per second, per video, per megapixel, per 1000 tokens) comparable. Where a model bills per megapixel or per token, 720p at the endpoint's native frame rate is assumed. Audio-on tiers cost more; the figure shown is audio-off where the two differ.

Lifecycle. Includes endpoints fal no longer lists in its public model index but still serves — the whole Veo 3.1 family (unlisted since Sep 2026), plus deprecated Kling 1.x–2.1, Veo 3, Luma Ray 2 and LTX-2 19B endpoints — each confirmed live against its OpenAPI schema and model page. The fal status filter separates them: 16 endpoints carry a dated deprecation notice from fal (Veo 3.1 on 17 Nov 2026, Luma Ray 2 on 25 Oct 2026) and 20 are already marked deprecated. Sora 2 is gone: OpenAI removed the Sora API on 24 Sep 2026 and fal's endpoints no longer resolve.

Promotional prices are recorded at the regular rate fal says applies afterwards (MiniMax H3-Max / H3-Max Turbo promo ends 15 Oct; Vidu Q4 promo ends 30 Nov); the promo rate and end date are in the expanded row.

12 endpoints are billed per GPU compute-second rather than per output, so no fixed rate exists — these are the legacy open models (SVD, AnimateDiff, SadTalker, MuseTalk, LivePortrait, T2V-Turbo and friends). Max frames is blank where output length simply follows the driving audio or video.
