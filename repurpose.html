All 199 video-to-video endpoints — everything that takes finished footage and changes it: extend, edit and restyle, inpaint and erase, lipsync, upscale and restore, interpolate, generate matching audio, or analyse. Models that create video from scratch are on the Video tab. Pricing is normalised to USD per minute of footage processed at 720p.

Method. Endpoint list from fal's model index (category video-to-video, all pages). Fields read from each endpoint's published openapi.json; pricing from fal's own copy, scraped from the model page where the metadata carries none.

Read $/min as a processing rate. Unlike the generation tab, this is the cost to run a minute of footage through the model, not the cost to create a minute. Per-frame billing (background removal, SAM) is converted at a 30 fps reference. Upscalers whose lowest tier is 1080p are priced at that tier and flagged in the expanded row.

16 endpoints are billed per GPU compute-second and show as compute-billed. Many columns here describe the model's output envelope — for pure analysis and utility endpoints (SAM, depth, ffmpeg) several are simply not applicable.
