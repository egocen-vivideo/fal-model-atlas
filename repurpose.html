Method. Endpoint list from fal's model index (category video-to-video, all pages). Fields read from each endpoint's published openapi.json; pricing from fal's own copy, scraped from the model page where the metadata carries none.

Read $/min as a processing rate. Unlike the generation tab, this is the cost to run a minute of footage through the model, not the cost to create a minute. Per-frame billing (background removal, SAM) is converted at a 30 fps reference. Upscalers whose lowest tier is 1080p are priced at that tier and flagged in the expanded row.

18 endpoints are billed per GPU compute-second and show as compute-billed. 7 carry a dated fal deprecation notice (Luma Ray 2 modify/reframe on 25 Oct, Veo 3.1 extend on 17 Nov, Lucy Edit Pro on 22 Oct) — see the fal status filter. Many columns here describe the model's output envelope — for pure analysis and utility endpoints (SAM, depth, ffmpeg) several are simply not applicable.
