Method. Endpoint list from fal's model index (/api/models, categories text-to-video + image-to-video, all pages). Duration, resolution, aspect-ratio, frame-count, audio and start/end-frame fields read from each endpoint's published openapi.json. Pricing from fal's own per-model pricing copy, normalised to a 60-second 720p render.

Read $/min as a rate, not a shopping cart. Most of these models cap out at 5–15 seconds, so a full minute is not a single generation — the column exists to make wildly different billing units (per second, per video, per megapixel, per 1000 tokens) comparable. Where a model bills per megapixel or per token, 720p at the endpoint's native frame rate is assumed. Audio-on tiers cost more; the figure shown is audio-off where the two differ.

12 endpoints are billed per GPU compute-second rather than per output, so no fixed rate exists — these are the legacy open models (SVD, AnimateDiff, SadTalker, MuseTalk, LivePortrait, T2V-Turbo and friends). Max frames is blank where output length simply follows the driving audio or video.
